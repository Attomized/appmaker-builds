Settings

Only player stats need a key. All other Fortnite-API sections work without it.

Get a free key from dash.fortnite-api.com. Do not enter your Epic account password here; this field is for a Fortnite-API key only.

Default: store the key only for this browser tab/session when possible. If you choose “Remember”, the key remains in local storage until you clear it. Avoid that on shared devices. Stats queries send the key directly to Fortnite-API over HTTPS.
